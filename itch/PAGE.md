## Description (Markdown)

```markdown
# Plan your movie marathon in a minute

Type in the movies, set how much time you have, and get a plan: what to watch in what order,
when each movie starts and ends, and when the night is over.

No account. No sign-up. Your list stays on your device.

## What it does

- **Fills your night.** "Best fit" picks the combination of movies that uses your time best.
  Got 5 hours? It finds the movies that actually fit, and tells you what didn't.
- **Four ways to order it.** Shortest first, longest first, balanced, or best fit.
- **Don't like the order? Shuffle it.** Or drag movies around, and pin the one you want to keep in place.
- **Looks up runtimes for you.** Start typing a title and pick it from the list.
- **Real-world details.** Break length, extra minutes per movie for trailers and credits, and a start time.
- **Now Playing mode.** A countdown for the current movie and the break, what's next, and when you'll finish.
  Start late? The times shift with you.
- **Share it.** Send a link or copy the plan as text for your group chat. Add it to your calendar. Print it for the fridge.
- **Keep several marathons.** "Halloween night", "Studio Ghibli Sunday", whatever you like. Back them up to a file.
- **Themes for the season.** It switches by date: Halloween, Christmas, Valentine's, St. Patrick's, 4th of July,
  Thanksgiving and the four seasons. You can also pick one yourself. Light and dark mode and larger text are included.

## How to use it

1. Type a movie title and pick it from the list. Its runtime fills in. Or type the time yourself.
2. Add the rest of your movies.
3. Set your break length, start time and, if you like, how many hours you have.
4. Pick an order, or hit **Shuffle** until it feels right.
5. Press **Start marathon** and go.

## Download or play

- **Play in your browser** with the button above. It also works offline once you install it
  from your browser's menu (Chrome and Edge, and on phones too).
- **Windows installer** in the downloads below. It isn't code-signed, so Windows may say
  "unknown publisher". Choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## Good to know

- The runtime lookup needs an internet connection and uses Wikidata. Some films have no runtime there,
  so you may need to type it.
- Your marathons are saved in the browser or app on the device you use. Use **Settings > Export marathons**
  to move them or keep a backup.

## More horror

Looking for something to put in the plan? Try **[HorrorHub](https://sarindre.itch.io/horrorhub)**,
your personal horror library.

Free and open source: https://github.com/sarindre/movie-marathon
```
